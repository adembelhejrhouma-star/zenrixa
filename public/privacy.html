Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Zenrixa ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up, we collect your name and email address, either entered directly or provided by Google when you sign in with your Google account.
  • Purchase information: When you buy a plan, we store the plan selected, billing cycle, and a reference to the payment transaction processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your card details.
  • Usage information: Basic technical data such as browser type and general usage patterns, used to improve the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process purchases and send related confirmations
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve and secure our platform

3. Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google, in accordance with the permissions you approve on Google's consent screen. We do not access any other Google account data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted service providers necessary to operate Zenrixa, such as our authentication provider (Supabase) and payment processor (Stripe), each bound by their own privacy and security obligations.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. While no system is 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.
